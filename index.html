About the club

Organized to help members navigate their taxes

Evergreen Tax Club, organized in 2021, is a nonprofit 501(c)(3) association formed exclusively for charitable and educational purposes: assisting members in identifying and resolving tax compliance, reviewing tax returns, tax planning and audit issues, and providing free tax help for low-income families in their preferred languages.

The club consists of individual tax professionals — CPAs and Enrolled Agents employed by corporations and other businesses. The principal function of our organization is providing educational and networking events for our members, connecting them with tax consultants and support from experienced tax professionals.
