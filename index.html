What we help with

Four things the club does for the people it serves

01

Tax compliance

We find and resolve compliance problems while they are still small — before penalties, interest, or a notice from the IRS turn them into something expensive.

02

Return reviews

A second set of credentialed eyes on a return that has already been filed. Rushed and self-prepared returns are where missed deductions hide.

03

Planning and audits

Planning for the year ahead, and steady guidance through an audit if one arrives. You will not face the correspondence alone.

04

Free help for families

Low-income families file at no cost, working with a member who speaks their preferred language. This is the charitable purpose the club was organized around.

About the club

Tax professionals, organized around a charitable purpose

Evergreen Tax Club was organized in 2021 as a nonprofit 501(c)(3) association, formed exclusively for charitable and educational purposes.

Its members are individual tax professionals — CPAs and Enrolled Agents working inside corporations and other businesses. The club runs educational and networking events for them, and connects them with experienced tax consultants when a problem needs a second opinion.

That professional network is what makes the free family program possible. The expertise is already in the room.
