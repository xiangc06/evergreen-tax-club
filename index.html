How it works

A careful path from first question to finished return

  1. 1

    Tell us what you need

    Send a basic inquiry without sensitive tax information.

  2. 2

    Confirm fit and eligibility

    We clarify the service, language, timing, cost, and who will help.

  3. 3

    Share documents securely

    If we proceed, you receive instructions for the approved secure exchange method.

  4. 4

    Prepare and review

    A tax professional prepares or reviews the work and explains anything needing attention.

  5. 5

    Approve the next step

    You review the result before filing, amendment, or any response is completed.
