About the club Organized 2021 · Bellevue, Washington

A club built by tax professionals, for the people they serve.

Evergreen Tax Club gathers CPAs and Enrolled Agents around one shared purpose: making the tax system navigable for the members of the club and for the families in their community.

Why the club exists

Organized in 2021 for charitable and educational purposes — to help members resolve tax problems, and to put professional tax help within reach of families who could not otherwise pay for it.

The work

Two halves of one organization

For its members, the club identifies and resolves tax compliance issues, reviews filed returns, and provides support on planning and audit matters. It runs educational and networking events, and connects members with tax consultants and experienced professionals when a question needs more than one opinion.

For the community, the club provides free tax help to low-income families in their preferred languages. Members volunteer their credentials and their time, which is what allows the program to cost families nothing.

The two halves depend on each other. A strong professional network is what makes the free program credible, and the free program is what gives the network its charitable purpose.

Membership

Who belongs to the club

Members are individual tax professionals — Certified Public Accountants and Enrolled Agents — employed by corporations and other businesses across the Puget Sound region.

Members can have their own prior returns reviewed at no cost, and gain access to the club's education programme and consultant network.
