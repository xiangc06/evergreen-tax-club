About us

A club built by tax professionals, for the people they serve

Evergreen Tax Club brings together CPAs and Enrolled Agents around a shared, charitable purpose: helping members and their communities handle taxes with confidence.

Organized in 2021 as a nonprofit 501(c)(3) association, exclusively for charitable and educational purposes.

Evergreen Tax Club assists its members in identifying and resolving tax compliance, reviewing tax returns, tax planning and audit issues, and provides free tax help for low-income families in their preferred languages.

The club consists of individual tax professionals — CPAs and Enrolled Agents employed by corporations and other businesses. The principal function of our organization is providing educational and networking events for our members, connecting them with tax consultants and support from experienced tax professionals.
